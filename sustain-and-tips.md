# 🩸 RS3 Beginner Survival & Sustain Guide (July 2026)
> A comprehensive guide to keeping your health up, skilling sustainably, and navigating RS3 as a fresh player.

---

## ⚔️ Combat Healing & Sustain

One of the biggest hurdles for new players is running out of food or losing adrenaline from eating during combat. In RS3, eating solid food (like Shark or Rocktail) drains **10% adrenaline** per bite, which slows down your ability to use powerful spenders and ultimates.

Here is how you keep your health up efficiently:

### 1. Adrenaline-Free Healing
To maintain high damage output, rely on items and food that do not drain adrenaline:
* **Blue Jellyfish:** Can be eaten in three bites, healing 750 HP per bite **without draining any adrenaline**.
* **Saradomin Brews (Potions):** Heals you over 6 sips without draining adrenaline, but lowers your combat stats slightly (restore stats with a Super Restore potion).
* **Tick Eating (The Combo):** You can eat a **Blue Jellyfish** and drink a **Saradomin Brew** in the exact same tick. This heals you for over 1,750+ HP instantly with zero adrenaline loss.

### 2. Passive Healing (Auras, Pocket, and Necklaces)
* **Vampyrism Aura:** Bought with Loyalty Points. Heals you for **5% of the damage you deal** (up to 50 HP per hitsplat). Essential for long combat sessions.
* **Blood Amulet of Fury:** A mid-game amulet that passively drains health from nearby targets and heals you every few seconds. Great for slayer and mob-heavy training.
* **Necromancy Ghost Conjure:** If playing Necromancy, your **Vengeful Ghost** automatically heals you for a portion of the damage it deals. This makes Necromancy the cheapest style to train.
* **Soul Split (Prayer 92):** The ultimate endgame heal. Actively heals you for 10% of all damage dealt.

### 3. Essential Pocket Item: Enhanced Excalibur
* **What it does:** Activating it (right-click in inventory or place on keybind) heals **10% of your maximum health every 4 seconds for 40 seconds** (totaling 100% of your health) with **zero adrenaline cost**.
* **How to get:** Complete the **Seers' Village Hard Achievements Diary**.
* *Note:* Keep this in your inventory at all times. It has a 5-minute cooldown but requires zero supplies.

## 🏃 Skilling Sustainability (Staying Alive While Skilling)

Certain skilling activities—especially **Thieving (pickpocketing)** and **Agility (failing obstacles)**—deal constant damage to your character. 

### How to Skill Without Dying:
1. **Regen Bracelet:** Doubles your natural hitpoint regeneration rate. Very cheap to buy on the Grand Exchange (GE) and has no requirements to wear.
2. **Rapid Renewal (Prayer 65):** Rapidly accelerates your HP regeneration. Pairs perfectly with the Regen Bracelet.
3. **Hitpoints Cape (Level 99):** Doubles natural HP regeneration. Stacks with the Regen Bracelet (giving 4x normal regeneration rate).
4. **Enhanced Excalibur:** Activate it every time it is off cooldown while pickpocketing to completely offset damage from failing.
5. **Elven Ritual Shard:** Similar to Excalibur, but restores **prayer points** over time. Useful if you are using health-boosting prayers while skilling.

## 🗺️ General Things Every Fresh Player Must Know

RS3 is a massive game with over 20 years of content. Here are the most important things to focus on as a brand new avatar:

### 1. Lodestones are Priority #1
Before doing anything else, run around Gielinor and unlock all **Lodestones** (the teleport circles). This gives you permanent, free teleports to almost every city in the game.

### 2. Fill Your Toolbelt
You do not need to carry a hatchet, pickaxe, hammer, or tinderbox in your inventory. 
* Right-click these items and select **Add to Toolbelt**. 
* They will reside there permanently and scale automatically when you upgrade them.

### 3. Questing is the True Key to Progression
Quests in RS3 are not just for lore; they unlock critical content and massive chunks of experience. Prioritize unlocking:
* **The Blood Pact:** Basic combat lamps and weapon setups.
* **Plague's End:** Unlocks **Prifddinas** (the high-level elven city, which is the hub for endgame skilling).
* **The Temple at Senntisten:** Unlocks Ancient Curses (Soul Split & Curses).
* **The World Wakes:** Unlocks the best combat ultimate abilities (like Death's Swiftness).

## 🖥️ Essential Settings & UI Layout

The default RS3 interface can be overwhelming. Here is how to configure it for clean gameplay:

### 1. Unlock the Interface
* Press **Esc** → `Edit Layout Mode`.
* Uncheck **Lock Interface** to drag, resize, and group tabs (like separating your Backpack, Prayer Book, and Ability Book into separate visible windows).
* Once satisfied, check **Lock Interface** again so you don't accidentally drag windows during combat.

### 2. Enable Revolution Combat
* Go to `Settings` → `Gameplay` → `Combat & Action Bar` → `Combat Mode` → select **Revolution**.
* Revolution automatically triggers your basic combat abilities for you so you can focus on movement and eating.
* Right-click the settings cog on your active action bar and choose **Auto-setup action bar** to get a workable bar instantly.
