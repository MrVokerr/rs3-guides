> Last updated: July 2026 | Verified against 2026 Combat Modernisation (EoC 2.0) Changes

## ⚡ The 2026 Ranged Identity (Post-EoC 2.0)

On **March 2, 2026**, Jagex released the **Combat Style Modernisation** (commonly referred to as EoC 2.0). Ranged was completely redesigned to center on **high-frequency multi-hits** and **triggering on-hit effects**. 

### Major Modernisation Changes
* **120 Cap Extension** — Ranged skill level now scales up to 120 with updated damage multipliers.
* **Ability Cleanup (9 Abilities Removed)** — To eliminate rotation bloat, the following abilities were retired/removed:
  * *Needle Strike, Dazing Shot, Salt the Wound, Fragmentation Shot, Tight Bindings, Demoralise, Rout, Unload, and Incendiary Shot.*
* **New Imbue Mechanics** — Introduces utility spells to match Ranged's on-hit theme:
  * **Imbue: Shadows** *(Replaces Incendiary Shot)*: Buffs you to generate **+5% adrenaline per hitsplat** for a short duration. Excellent for hyper-charging spenders.
  * **Imbue: Gales**: Grants a flat **damage bonus per hitsplat** for a short duration. Pairs perfectly with rapid-fire multi-hit abilities.
* **No More Switch-scape** — Traditional threshold requirements are retired. The Planted Feet weapon switch is gone; Greater Death's Swiftness now natively extends your damage buff duration.
* **Auras Disabled/Retired** — Offensive combat auras like *Reckless* and *Sharpshooter* are retired. Their damage bonuses are now built natively into Ranged level scaling. Use utility/sustain auras like **Vampyrism** or **Penance** instead.

> [!WARNING]
> **2026 Meta Commentary:** While the modernization cleanup removed rotation bloat, Ranged in July 2026 still has a high "mental tax" and high upkeep costs. This is due to managing multiple arrow types (Ful vs Wen), active Essence of Finality (EoF) special attack switches (such as Seren Godbow), and the high GP cost of consuming Elder God arrows. It is a highly rewarding, high-DPS style but requires active attention.

## ⚙️ Core Combat Mechanics & Settings

To optimize your Ranged training, configure these essential settings and understand how XP and damage scale:

### 1. Combat XP Scaling
* **Formula:** You earn **50 combat experience for every 1,000 damage dealt** (equivalent to 5% of damage).
* **XP Splitting:** If you split your XP with Defense, you will get 25 Ranged and 25 Defense XP per 1,000 damage.
* **Setting Config:** Go to `Settings` → `Gameplay` → `Combat & Action Bar` → `Combat XP` → select either **Ranged**, **Defense**, or **Both**.
* *Note:* Constitution (HP) experience is gained separately through all combat styles automatically.

### 2. Accuracy & Target Information
* Your accuracy is determined by your Ranged level, weapon tier, weapon style, and gear bonuses. 
* Accuracy determines how much of your maximum damage you deal. For example, if your max hit is 100 but your target accuracy is 75%, your effective max hit is capped at 75.
* **Setting Config:** Go to `Settings` → `Gameplay` → `Combat & Action Bar` → `Targeting` → enable **Target Information** to see exact accuracy/hit chance on monsters.

### 3. Exploiting Weaknesses
Monsters have specific weaknesses to weapon types:
* **Arrows:** Use a Bow.
* **Bolts:** Use a Crossbow (Dual-wield or 2H).
* **Thrown:** Use Darts, Javelins, Chinchompas, etc.
* Matching target weaknesses significantly boosts your combat accuracy.

### 4. Experience Boosters & QoL Commands
* **In-Game Search:** Type `/wiki <item_name>` in chat to automatically load the official wiki page for any item.
* **Scrimshaw of Sacrifice:** Extremely powerful boost that increases combat experience by **50%**, but **destroys all monster drops**. Currently costs ~12M GP.
* **Lowe's Archery Store (Varrock):** Best location for new players to buy basic bows, crossbows, and ammunition.

## 🗺️ Level-by-Level Ranged Training Route & Gear Setups

Here is the exact leveling route, combined with the precise gear and ammunition setups recommended for each level bracket.

### 🟢 1. Levels 1-20: Troll Shamans
* **Target:** Troll Shamans
* **Location:** Burthorpe Troll Cave (walk north from Burthorpe or Taverley lodestone).
* **XP Rate:** ~15k - 25k XP/hr
* **Method:** Fully AFK. Auto-retaliate active.
* **Recommended Gear Setup:**
  * **Armor:** Leather Body, Leather Chaps, Leather Vambraces.
  * **Weapon:** Basic Shortbow or Oak Shortbow.
  * **Ammunition:** Bronze Arrows.
  * *Note:* Speak to Lowe in Varrock to purchase these early items. If you need a starting ability bar, click the settings cog on the action bar and choose **Auto-setup**.

---

### 🟢 2. Levels 20-30: Warped Bats
* **Target:** Warped Bats
* **Location:** Lumbridge Catacombs (first rooms).
* **Prerequisite:** Complete the quick <a href="https://runescape.wiki/w/The_Blood_Pact" target="_blank">The Blood Pact</a> quest at the Lumbridge Catacombs entrance. This unlocks access to the training area and provides starter weapons and XP lamps.
* **XP Rate:** ~30k - 45k XP/hr
* **Method:** AFK.
* **Recommended Gear Setup:**
  * **Armor:** Leather or Spined Armour (if Defense allows).
  * **Weapon:** Willow Shortbow (Level 20 Bow).
  * **Ammunition:** Iron Arrows.

---

### 🟢 3. Levels 30-40: Corpse Mages
* **Target:** Corpse Mages
* **Location:** Lumbridge Catacombs (deeper rooms).
* **XP Rate:** ~45k - 70k XP/hr
* **Method:** AFK.
* **Key Ability Unlock:** At **Level 35**, you unlock **Bombardment** (AoE ability hitting your target and up to 9 other enemies in a 5x5 area). Place this early on your action bar to clear groups fast.
* **Recommended Gear Setup:**
  * **Armor:** Spined Armour or Blue Dragonhide (requires Level 30 Defense).
  * **Weapon:** Maple Shortbow (Level 30 Bow).
  * **Ammunition:** Steel Arrows.

---

### 🟢 4. Levels 40-50/60: Catablepons
* **Target:** Catablepons (weak to arrows)
* **Location:** Stronghold of Security (3rd layer/green level, west of Varrock).
* **XP Rate:** ~80k - 120k XP/hr
* **Method:** Semi-AFK. Pass the security doors once, then use the portal shortcut at the entrance to skip down on return trips.
* **Recommended Gear Setup:**
  * **Armor:** Blue Dragonhide (Level 30 Def) or Red Dragonhide (Level 50 Def).
  * **Weapon:** Yew Shortbow (Level 40 Bow) → Magic Shortbow (Level 50 Bow).
  * **Ammunition:** Adamant Arrows (with Yew Bow) → Runite Arrows (with Magic Bow).

#### 💡 Alternative Mid-Game Paths:
* **Option A (Lesser Demons - Levels 50-60):** Target Lesser Demons at Karamja Volcano (Port Sarim boat to Karamja). They are weak to bolts. Use a **Rune 2H Crossbow** with **Runite Bolts** and Blue/Red Dragonhide armor.
* **Option B (Pyrefiends - Levels 45-60):** Requires 30 Slayer. Target Pyrefiends in the Fremennik Slayer Dungeon. Use **Chinchompas** (main hand) + **Off-hand Rune Crossbow** (off hand) with Red Dragonhide. Chinchompas act as AoE ammunition hitting in a 3x3 grid. Pull mobs together using an *Aggression Potion*.

---

### 🔵 5. Levels 50-70: The Abyss
* **Target:** Abyssal Walkers, Guardians, and Leeches
* **Location:** Wilderness Abyss Inner Ring (accessed via the Zamorak Mage north of Edgeville).
* **Prerequisite:** Complete the Abyss miniquest.
* **XP Rate:** ~150k - 250k XP/hr
* **Method:** Semi-AFK. Stand in the **southwestern spot** for optimal spawn grouping. reset aggression after 10 minutes by running out of the room and back.
* **Recommended Gear Setup:**
  * **Armor:** Red Dragonhide (Level 50 Def) → Black Dragonhide (Level 60 Def) or Armadyl Power Armour (Level 70 Def).
  * **Weapon:** Red Chinchompas (main hand) + Off-hand Rune/Dragon Crossbow (off hand).
  * **Ammunition:** Red Chinchompas (MH) + Runite/Broad Bolts (OH).
  * **Pocket Slot:** Scripture of Jas or Wen (great budget options for AoE).
  * **Aura:** Vampyrism Aura (for constant passive healing during the mob onslaught).

#### 💡 Alternative Wilderness Path (Levels 60-70):
* **Target:** Greater Demons at the Sword of Edicts (Wilderness).
* **Important:** Speak to **Vala** near the Edgeville Lodestone to **turn off PvP** before entering.
* **Recommended Gear Setup:** Red Salamander (T60) using Tarromin Tar as ammunition, or dual-wield Demon Slayer Crossbows using Broad-tipped Bolts. Armored in Black Dragonhide.

---

### 🔵 6. Levels 70-99: Waterfiends or Shattered Worlds
* **Target Option A:** Waterfiends (weak to arrows, Waterbirth Island dungeon via Rellekka dock). Very AFK at higher levels.
* **Target Option B:** Shattered Worlds (Lumbridge Swamp Portal). Active, high intensity, but yields peak mid-game XP.
* **XP Rate:** ~300k - 350k XP/hr (Waterfiends) | **~800k - 1.2M+ XP/hr** (Shattered Worlds)
* **Recommended Gear Setup:**
  * **Armor:** Armadyl Power Armour (T70) → Sirenic Armour (T90, degradable but highly cost-effective).
  * **Weapon (Waterfiends):** Crystal Bow (T70) → Decimation Bow (T87) or Noxious Longbow (T90).
  * **Weapon (Shattered Worlds):** Mechanised Chinchompas (main hand) + Off-hand Shadow Glaive or Off-hand Ascension Crossbow (off hand).
  * **Ammunition:** Ful Arrows or Wen Arrows (for Bows) | Mechanised Chinchompas + Ascension/Broad Bolts (for Glaive/Crossbow setups).
  * **Aura:** Vampyrism or Penance.
  * **Pocket Slot:** Scripture of Ful (+15% damage boost) or Scripture of Wen.
  * **Accessories:** **Blood Fury Amulet** (essential for AFK health recovery), Nightmare Gauntlets (T85), Fleeting Boots (T85), Pernix's Quiver.
  * **Key Unlocks:** Activate **Death's Swiftness** (World Wakes quest) and **Corruption Shot** (Mazcab Codex) to melt groups.

### 🔴 7. Levels 99-120: Peak Endgame Methods

Select your path based on your preferred intensity and playstyle:

#### ⚔️ Active Peak: Armoured Phantoms (~1.9M - 2.1M XP/hr)
* **Location:** Fort Forinthry Catacombs.
* **Recommended Gear Setup:**
  * **Armor:** Elite Dracolich Armour (T92, crafted with Vorkath Spikes + Sirenic scales).
  * **Weapon:** Bow of the Last Guardian (BoLG) T95, upgraded to T100 using a Shard of Genesis Essence.
  * **Ammunition:** **Wen Arrows** (high defense target) or **Ful Arrows** (default high-damage camp).
  * **Pocket Slot:** Erethodor's Grimoire (raises crit cap, high upkeep) or Scripture of Ful.
  * **Aura:** Supreme Sharpshooter Aura (boosts accuracy significantly).
  * **Relics:** Conservation of Energy + Fury of the Small.
  * **Defense:** Keep *Darkness* spell and Soul Split active.

#### 💤 AFK Peak: Abyssal Savages (~600k - 700k+ XP/hr)
* **Location:** Wilderness (ensure PvP is toggled off at Vala in Edgeville).
* **Recommended Gear Setup:**
  * **Armor:** Elite Dracolich or Sirenic Armour.
  * **Weapon:** Bow of the Last Guardian (BoLG) or Noxious Longbow.
  * **Ammunition:** **Jas Demonbane Arrows** (gives massive damage multipliers against demons).
  * **Pocket Slot:** Scripture of Ful or Wen.
  * **Aura:** Penance Aura or Penance Powder (maintains prayer points permanently).
  * **Accessories:** Cinderbane Gloves + Weapon Poison+++ (melts target HP via poison damage).

## ⚡ Ability Bar Setups (Revo vs Manual)

With the 2026 modernisation, you can automatically set up a basic Ranged bar by right-clicking the cog icon on your action bar and selecting **Auto-setup action bar**.

For optimal performance, the community recommends a **"Basics Only"** Revolution bar. This automates your basic skills to build adrenaline, leaving high-impact Spenders and Ultimates to be triggered manually.

### 🔄 2-Handed Bow Basics Only Bar
`[Greater Ricochet / Ricochet] → [Shadow Tendrils] → [Imbue: Shadows] → [Imbue: Gales] → [Piercing Shot] → [Snipe]`

### 🔄 Dual-Wield Crossbow Basics Only Bar
`[Greater Ricochet / Ricochet] → [Needle Strike] → [Imbue: Shadows] → [Imbue: Gales] → [Binding Shot] → [Snipe]`

*   **Revo++ Full Automation Option (Single-Target Bow):** If you prefer fully hands-free combat without manual inputs:
    `[Death's Swiftness] → [Imbue: Shadows] → [Imbue: Gales] → [Rapid Fire] → [Greater Ricochet / Ricochet] → [Snapshot] → [Piercing Shot]`

> [!TIP]
> **Rotation Logic:** Always keep **Imbue: Shadows** and **Imbue: Gales** immediately before multi-hit spenders like **Rapid Fire** and **Greater Ricochet**. *Shadows* will generate massive adrenaline, while *Gales* adds flat damage to every single hit splat.

## 🏹 Ammunition Meta: Bows vs Crossbows

Bows completely dominate the 2026 endgame meta. Crossbows and Bakriminel bolts are no longer viable for high-tier PvM due to the raw power of Elder God arrows.

### The Arrow Loadout (Use Pernix's Quiver)
* **Ful Arrows** — +15% flat damage boost at the cost of -10% accuracy. Camp this as your default arrow type if you have 100% accuracy on the target.
* **Wen Arrows** — Stacks accuracy and damage bonuses on-hit. Excellent for high-defense targets.
* **Jas Demonbane Arrows** — Unlocks massive damage multipliers against demons (crucial for Abyssal Savages/Beasts training).
* **Bik Arrows** — Applies stacking poison. Pairs with Cinderbane Gloves + Weapon Poison+++ to melt poison-vulnerable bosses.
* **Deathspore Arrows** — Build critical stacks to execute a free spender/ultimate ability.
* **Splintering Arrows** — Stacks the *Puncture* debuff up to 250 times for high sustained bleed damage.

## 🎒 Best Pocket, Cape, Auras, and Archaeology Relics

* **Pocket Slot** — **Scripture of Ful** (+15% damage passive, cost-effective) or **Erethodor's Grimoire** (raises critical cap, high upkeep cost). Use **Scripture of Wen** or **Amascut** for budget AoE leveling.
* **Cape Slot (Igneous Kal-Xil)** — Empowered by defeating **TzKal-Zuk**. It reduces the adrenaline cost of the **Deadshot** ultimate ability to **60%** and causes it to hit **8 times** (delivering 55-75% ability damage per hit). This massive multi-hit fits perfectly with the Dracolich Infusion set effect and on-hit buffs.
* **Sustain Auras** — Use **Vampyrism Aura** (+5% lifesteal, capped at 50 HP/hit) or **Penance Aura** (restores prayer when taking damage) since combat auras are disabled/retired.
* **Archaeology Relics** — Equip **Conservation of Energy** (120 Arch, refunds 10% adrenaline on ultimates) and **Fury of the Small** (97 Arch, basic abilities give +1% adrenaline) for optimized rotations.

## 💪 Supporting Skills to Train Alongside Ranged

Ranged DPS in 2026 is high-upkeep and high-reward. Leveling Ranged alone is not enough — you need a stack of support skills, quests, and ability unlocks to perform at a proficient level. These aren't optional for serious progression.

### Non-Negotiables (Do These First)

| Skill / Unlock | Target Level | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Prayer** | **95** ← Critical | Unlocks **Anguish** (+10% ranged damage, accuracy, and defence) and **Soul Split** (92, self-healing). Both require **The Temple at Senntisten**. Non-negotiable for endgame. |
| **Herblore** | **96–99** ← Critical | **Overload** (96) is the single biggest combat buff outside gear. At 99, unlock **Supreme Overload Salves** and other endgame potions (adrenaline renewals, etc.). |
| **Defence** | **70 → 90** | Unlocks armor tiers: Armadyl (70) → Sirenic (90) → Elite Dracolich (92). Train alongside Ranged or you'll cap your gear potential. |
| **Invention** | Unlock ASAP (80 Div, 80 Craft, 80 Smith) | Augment gear with perks — **Precise 6, Biting 4, Caroming, Aftershock, Relentless, Impatient**, etc. Massive damage increase. |

### The Non-Negotiable Priority Order

```
Prayer 95 → Herblore 96+ → Invention unlock → Defence to match your gear tier
```

Do not let anything distract you from Prayer 95 and Herblore 96+. Every hour of bossing without **Soul Split**, **Anguish**, and **Overloads** is significantly less effective.

### What "Proficient" Actually Looks Like

**Minimum competent ranged DPS:**
- 99 Ranged, 80+ Defence, 95 Prayer, 96 Herblore, Invention unlocked with basic perks
- Death's Swiftness + Greater Ricochet
- T90 bow (Noxious) or better, Armadyl/Sirenic armor
- Overloads + Anguish + Soul Split active

**Strong / endgame proficient:**
- 120 Ranged, 99 Defence, 99 Herblore, high Invention with BiS perk setups
- BoLG or Noxious + Elite Dracolich
- Archaeology relics slotted (Fury of the Small + Conservation of Energy)
- Elder God arrows (Ful/Wen), Essence of Finality with SGB spec, Igneous Kal-Xil cape
- Comfortable manual rotation: **Imbue: Shadows/Gales → Rapid Fire / Greater Ricochet** inside Death's Swiftness windows

### Quick Mental Model

Your side skills solve three problems:

1. **Raw damage** — Herblore (Overloads), Prayer (Anguish), Invention (perks)
2. **Survivability** — Prayer (Soul Split), Defence (armor tiers)
3. **Rotation economy** — Archaeology relics, Greater Ricochet, Impatient/Relentless perks

> [!WARNING]
> If you're currently leveling Ranged, train **Defence alongside it** (split XP or alternate) and start pushing **Prayer toward 95** as soon as you can handle **The Temple at Senntisten**. Don't sink millions into T90 gear before you have Overloads and Ancient Curses — that's the most common mistake new ranged players make.
