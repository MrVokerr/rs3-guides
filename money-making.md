> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Margin Testing Rule:**
> GE guide prices move daily. Before committing to any method, always do a 1-item test run:
> * **Gathering/Combat:** Kill 1 monster, sell the drops instantly on the GE to see the *real* price.
> * **Processing:** Buy 1 raw material, process it, sell it back. Calculate your actual profit margin (remember the **2% GE tax**).
> * **Compare Variants:** Some methods have daily variation (e.g., different herb incense sticks, different dragonhide colours). Test multiple and pick the winner.

## 🟢 Phase 1: Starting from Absolute Zero (F2P / No Requirements)

These methods require no stats, no starting capital, and work in Free-to-Play.

### 1. Slaying Cows in Burthorpe (~956k GP/hour)
* **Location:** Burthorpe / Taverley cow pen (north of the Taverley lodestone — NOT Lumbridge).
* **Why Burthorpe?** The gnome banker is significantly closer than Lumbridge's cow pen.
* **Collect:** Cowhides (~673 GP) + Raw Beef (~985 GP). Bones (~146 GP) are optional — burying them frees inventory for more hides/beef.
* **Wiki rate:** ~540 kills/hr ≈ **~956k GP/hr** after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax.
* **Bonus:** Raw Beef can later be cooked into Sinews for more profit (see Phase 2).

### 2. Draynor Cabbage Picking (~1.5M GP/hour)
* **Location:** Cabbage field south of the Draynor Village farming patch.
* **Method:** Harvest cabbages (~1,422 GP each), bank at Draynor (F2P) or use Explorer's Ring cabbage-port + War's Retreat bank (members, faster).
* **Wiki rate:** ~1,100 cabbages/hr ≈ **~1.5M GP/hr** after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. after tax. Consistent demand from Player-Owned Farms and questing.

### 3. Spell Wisps at Wizard's Tower (~1M – 1.2M GP/hour)
* **Location:** Outside the Wizard's Tower (south of Draynor Village).
* **Method:** Kill Spell Wisps. Guaranteed drop: **Impious Ashes** (~1,911 GP each).
* **Banking:** Use the deposit NPC standing outside the tower — no walking back to Draynor needed.
* **Note:** Ash prices fluctuate; always check the live GE price before committing a long session.

### 4. Archaeology Material Farming (F2P, Capped at Level 20)
* **Location:** Kharid-et digsite.
* **Method:** Excavate low-level spots. Collect materials such as **Third Age Iron** (~2,474 GP), **Goldrune** (~2,336 GP), **Samite Silk** (~2,122 GP), and **Zarosian Insignia** (~4,751 GP). Fully AFK — just click and wait.
* **Banking:** Use the bank chest at the top of the digsite.
* **Note:** Material prices cooled from the Combat Modernisation launch spike earlier in 2026, but this remains solid passive income.

### 5. Mining Clay (F2P, Better Than Gem Rocks)
* **Location:** Burthorpe mines (southwest of the bank).
* **Method:** Mine clay (~1,014 GP) and sell on the GE. Equip a **Bracelet of Clay** to automatically soften it into soft clay (~1,235 GP) for higher value.
* **Wiki rate:** ~940 clay/hr ≈ **~934k GP/hr**; with bracelets ≈ **~1.3M GP/hr**.
* **Why not Gem Rocks?** Gem rocks have a 1min 25s respawn timer shared with other players. Clay is faster and more consistent.
* **P2P upgrade:** Soft clay in Prifddinas (Ithell, 75 Mining + Plague's End) is ~**4.2M GP/hr** — revisit once unlocked.

## 🟡 Phase 2: Low-to-Mid Stats Processing (1M–5M Starting Budget)

Once you have some starter GP, these processing methods scale your income dramatically.

### 1. Tanning Dragonhides (~8.4M – ~20M GP/hour)
* **Requirements:** Level 1 Crafting (any hide type works)
* **Intensity:** High click rate (most profitable active method at low levels)
* **Current margins (July 20, 2026):**

| Hide | Hide Price | Leather Price | Profit/ea (after tax + tan fee) | ~40k/hr | ~60k/hr |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Green | 568 | 814 | ~210 | ~8.4M | ~13M |
| Blue | 912 | 1,181 | ~225 | ~9M | ~14M |
| Black | 1,687 | 2,079 | ~330 | ~13M | ~20M |

* **Method:**
  1. Set up a bank preset: full inventory (28) of dragonhides.
  2. Use a **Portable Crafter** next to a bank chest. World 84 Fort Forinthry / Lumbridge Market usually has one deployed.
  3. Right-click configure the crafter → "Tan Leather".
  4. Loop: Load preset → Left-click crafter → Press Space → Bank → Repeat.
  5. Wiki peak efficiency: **~60,000 hides/hour** (stockpile past the 10k/4hr buy limit first).
* **2026 Boost:** Dragon leather demand remains elevated from the Havenhythe **Masterwork Ranged Armor** chain (Apex Leather).
* **Tip:** Test 1 hide + 1 leather first. If the spread is under ~200 GP, switch colours.

### 2. Cooking Raw Beef into Sinews (~2M – 2.7M GP/hour)
* **Requirements:** Level 1 Cooking
* **Current prices:** Raw Beef ~985 GP → Sinew ~**2,657 GP** (~1,619 GP profit after tax).
* **Method:** Buy raw beef on the GE → use on a range and choose **Make sinew** (not cook). Lumbridge Castle kitchen works well.
* **Why it works:** Sinews feed into crossbow strings and **Masterwork / Apex Sinew** demand from Havenhythe ranged armour.
* **Rate:** ~1,500–1,800 sinews/hr ≈ **~2.4M – 2.9M GP/hr**. Margins are thinner than the Havenhythe launch spike (sinews briefly hit 6–7k).
* **Warning:** You will burn meat if you accidentally cook instead of making sinew. Always select the sinew option. Do **not** use a fire — sinew only works on a range.

### 3. Molten Glass → Bomb Vials (~2.5M GP/hour + Crafting XP)
* **Requirements:** Level 54 Crafting (Bomb Vials) | Level 1 Crafting (Beer Glasses — **low profit**, skip)
* **Current prices:** Molten Glass ~394 GP → Bomb Vial ~**1,804 GP** (~1,374 GP profit after tax).
* **Wiki rate:** ~1,830 vials/hr ≈ **~2.5M GP/hr**.
* **Why Bomb Vials?** Permanently consumed in endgame PvM (Vulnerability Bombs, etc.) — stable demand.
* **Note:** Beer Glasses (~601 GP) only clear ~195 GP each after tax — not worth it as a primary method.

### 4. Incense Sticks (~5.8M – ~11M GP/hour)
* **Requirements:** Level 35–75 Firemaking (varies by stick type)
* **Method:** Buy ashy incense sticks + clean herbs on the GE → combine into finished incense sticks. **Quick-cense** (Garden of Kharid) roughly doubles output — highly recommended.
* **Current wiki rates (adding herbs, with Quick-cense):**

| Stick | Level | Approx. GP/hr |
|---|---|---|
| Spirit weed | 35 | ~11M |
| Kwuarm | 54 | ~5.8M |
| Lantadyme | 67 | ~8M |
| Torstol | 75 | ~9.3M |

* **Best Types to Test:** Spirit Weed, Torstol, Lantadyme, Kwuarm, Dwarf Weed. Margins change daily — always test 1 first.
* **Why it works:** Incense sticks are consumed in PvM and skilling. Always in demand.
* **2026 Note:** Prices spike during Double XP events. Stockpile materials before DXP and sell finished sticks during.
* **AFK Level:** High — great second-monitor activity. Ashy-stick buy volume can be thin; be patient on buy offers.

### 5. Fort Forinthry Construction (Refined Planks)
* **Requirements:** Level 40 Construction (complete the **New Foundations** quest for instant Level 40 + unlocks). Higher planks need higher Construction (Magic 80, Elder 90).
* **GP Rate:** ~**2M – 5.3M GP/hour** depending on plank tier (market-dependent).
* **Current wiki highlights:** Refined Magic planks ~**4.6M** | Refined Elder planks ~**5.3M**. Lower tiers (Willow/Maple) land closer to ~2–3M/hr.
* **Method:** Buy planks on the GE → process into Refined Planks at the Fort Forinthry sawmill (4 planks → 1 refined) → optionally process further into Wooden Frames (~24.1k each).
* **Strategy:** Stockpile frames *before* Double XP events, then slow-sell them for peak prices.

## 🔵 Phase 3: Mid-to-High Level Methods (P2P)

### 1. Necrotic Rune Crafting (~36M – ~52M GP/hour optimised) ⭐ TOP METHOD
* **Requirements:** Runecrafting (110+ for best multipliers), Impure Essence (~131 GP), Rune Mythos quest
* **Method:** Craft Spirit, Bone, Flesh, or Miasma necrotic runes at the Necromantic Rune Temple altars. Use an **Abyssal Titan** familiar for extra essence capacity.
* **Current wiki rates (full setup — pouches, ethereal outfit, powerbursts, Passing Bracelet, etc.):**

| Rune | Approx. GP/hr after tax |
|---|---|
| Spirit | ~**52M** |
| Bone | ~**41M** |
| Flesh | ~**39M** |
| Miasma | ~**36M** |

* **Why it dominates:** Necrotic runes are consumed by every Necromancy player — rituals, combat, everything. Demand is insatiable.
* **AFK Level:** Semi-active (altar runs), but exceptionally profitable.
* **Realistic expectation:** Without the full endgame setup, expect substantially less (often still **15M–30M+**). Always margin-test with your actual trip rate.
* **2026 Note:** Still one of the most consistent non-combat profit methods post-TH removal.

### 2. Fletching Onyx Bolts (~9.6M GP/hour GE, ~45M+ GP/hour via High Alch)
* **Requirements:** Level 73 Fletching
* **Current prices:** Onyx Bolt Tips ~**7,774** + Rune Bolts ~**174** → Onyx Bolts ~**6,683** GE | High Alch **8,179**.
* **Method:**
  1. Buy Rune Bolts + Onyx Bolt Tips on the GE.
  2. Join World 84. Use a **Portable Fletcher**. Equip **Fletching Cape** + **Brooch of the Gods**. Ranger's Workroom T2 helps.
  3. Attach tips to bolts. Passive buffs (cape, brooch, portable, workroom) create extra bolts beyond materials consumed.
  4. **Prefer High Alch** right now — GE guide price sits below alch value. Use an **Auto-Alchemiser Mk II** to automate, or alch manually.
* **Wiki GE rate:** ~**9.6M GP/hr** after tax (with extras). Alching the same output is far higher (~45M+ depending on extras and speed).
* **Buy Limit Warning:** Onyx Bolt Tips and Rune Bolts are 10k each / 4 hours. Slow-buy in advance.
* **2026 Note:** July's Fletching re-tier may change level requirements — monitor patch notes. Always re-check whether GE or alch is better before bulk buying.

### 3. Spinning Starbloom Flowers into Cloth (~8.9M GP/hour, Fully AFK)
* **Requirements:** Level 100 Crafting
* **Current prices:** Starbloom Flower ~**15,645** | Starbloom Thread ~**13,684** | Starbloom Cloth ~**46,883**.
* **Method (recommended):**
  1. Buy Starbloom Flowers → spin into **Starbloom Thread** at a spinning wheel (Fort Forinthry Ranger's Workroom, next to bank).
  2. Weave threads into **Starbloom Cloth** at a loom (2 threads = 1 cloth).
  3. Sell cloth. Wiki rate: 1,210 flowers → 605 cloth/hr ≈ **~8.9M GP/hr** after tax.
* **Important:** Spinning flowers into thread and selling the **thread** currently **loses money** (thread GE price is below flower price). Always finish into cloth.
* **Buy-limit tip:** Thread has almost no GE volume even though it looks cheap — buy flowers, not thread.
* **2026 Note:** Margins have tightened since introduction. Re-check flower vs cloth prices before bulk buying.

### 4. Slayer Tasks (Variable, Scales with Level)
* **Method:** Complete Slayer assignments, collect salvage drops and alchables.
* **GP Rate:** Scales massively with combat level. High-level tasks (Abyssal Demons, Rune Dragons) are commonly **5M–15M+ GP/hr**.
* **Tips:** Use Slayer masks to force profitable tasks. Necromancy makes clearing tasks very fast.
* **2026 Note:** Post-Combat Modernisation, Slayer is one of the best ways to simultaneously train combat to 120 AND make money.

### 5. Easy Clue Scrolls (~2M – 5M GP/hour)
* **Method:** Pickpocket H.A.M. Members (Lumbridge) to obtain Easy Clue Scrolls. Solve using the **Alt1 Toolkit**.
* **Why it works:** Rewards include Fortunate Component fodder (Bob shirts ~**978k** each, trimmed armour, etc.) that high-level players disassemble for Invention. Individual clue value varies wildly — expect streaks of dry and juicy clues.
* **Tools:** Alt1 Toolkit for instant puzzle solving. Join the **Clue Chasers** Discord for community help.
* **Note:** GP/hr is highly RNG-dependent; treat the range as a long-run average, not a guarantee.

## 💰 GE Flipping (Passive Income Layer)

* **Core Concept:** Buy items at the "slow buy" price → sell at the "insta buy" price. Pocket the margin.
* **2026 GE Changes:** Price shift cap increased from 5% → 20% (prices correct faster). Live trade data now shown. Factor in the **2% GE tax**.
* **Strategy:** Set buy/sell orders, then go do other activities. Diversify across 8 trade slots (members).
* **Pro Tip:** Flip items you can also *process* — e.g., if raw beef crashes, you can cook it into sinews instead of selling at a loss.
* **Watch For:** Update-driven price spikes (Havenhythe, Mid-Game Rebalance, DXP announcements).
* **Resources:** [GE Market Watch](https://runescape.wiki/w/Grand_Exchange_Market_Watch), [Money making guide](https://runescape.wiki/w/Money_making_guide), RS Wiki price trackers.
