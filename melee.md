## Why Melee in 2026

On **2 March 2026**, Jagex shipped **Combat Style Modernisation**. Melee was rebuilt around:

- **Level 120 Attack and Strength** (same cap as Necromancy / modernised Ranged / Magic)
- A **single Melee ability book** — the old Strength book is gone; abilities unlock from **Attack**
- **Bloodlust** — basics build stacks; spenders consume them for empowered damage
- **Bleed synergy** — one multi-cast bleed line: **Dismember → Slaughter → Massacre**, with built-in healing
- **No thresholds** — adrenaline spenders fire when you have the adrenaline
- **Weapon identity** — dual-wield and two-handed change ability behaviour (notably **Adaptive Strike** and **Flurry** vs **Hurricane**)

Melee’s identity is **burst + bleeds + on-kill / on-next-attack triggers**. It is the most weapon-dependent of the four styles.

> [!WARNING]
> Treat any guide as outdated if it still recommends **thresholds**, separate Attack/Strength ability books, or removed abilities such as **Quake, Smash, Cleave, Decimate, Sever, Slice, Destroy, Frenzy, Blood Tendrils, Havoc, Kick, Stomp, Forceful Backhand, Balanced Strike**.  
> The **entire aura slot** was removed in the [Aura Overhaul](https://runescape.wiki/w/Aura_Overhaul) (**13 April 2026**). Do **not** equip “Vampyrism aura” / “Penance aura” — those items are gone. Use the **[Aspect](https://runescape.wiki/w/Aspects)** spells instead (one Aspect at a time).  
> The Wiki’s Abyss training bar may still list **Quake** on the incomplete [Pay-to-play melee training](https://runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_melee_training) page — **do not** copy that.

## Core mechanics (read this once)

### Combat XP

- You earn **50 combat XP per 1,000 damage** dealt.
- Set XP in `Settings → Gameplay → Combat & Action Bar → Combat XP` to **Attack**, **Strength**, **Defence**, or a split.
- Constitution XP is gained separately from all styles.

### Bloodlust (the Melee loop)

Current Wiki rules ([Bloodlust](https://runescape.wiki/w/Bloodlust), checked July 2026):

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Cap | **4** stacks normally; **8** during **Berserk** |
| Build | Most **basic** Melee abilities generate **1** stack (**Bladed Dive** does not); **Rend** generates **2**; **Berserk** grants **4** on cast |
| Spend | At 4+ stacks, empower **Assault**, **Hurricane**, **Flurry**, or **Greater Flurry** |
| Berserk | Doubles Bloodlust from basics, raises cap to 8, melee damage **×1.75**, you take **×1.25** damage; shortens **Overpower** cooldown |

**Empowered effects (current):**

| Ability | Bloodlust consume effect |
|---------|--------------------------|
| **Assault** | Per-hit damage **130–150% → 170–190%** (channelled 4 hits) |
| **Hurricane** | Extra hit of **75–95%** to target + up to 9 nearby |
| **Flurry / Greater Flurry** | **+1% damage per 1% target HP missing**, up to **+65%** |

Practical loop: **basics → 4 Bloodlust → Assault / Hurricane / Flurry → repeat**. Inside **Berserk**, dump empowered spenders hard.

> [!TIP]
> On dual-wield, Wiki comparison ([Bloodlust](https://runescape.wiki/w/Bloodlust)): with Bloodlust, **Flurry** deals more total damage than **Assault** below ~**61–62%** target HP; if you plan to use both, spend Bloodlust on Flurry once the target is below ~**70%** HP.

### DW vs 2H (both tracks)

| Track | Strengths | Signature tools |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| **2H / halberd-type** | Best **AoE training**; **Adaptive Strike** cone + **Hurricane**; longer reach on spears/scythes/halberds | Halberds → Crystal Halberd → Laniakea’s Spear → Noxious Scythe → (**Tumeken's Light** if affordable) → Masterwork 2H / Ek-ZekKil |
| **Dual-wield** | Strong **single-target** burst; **Flurry / Greater Flurry**; flexible MH/OH | Whip + OH → Drygores → Abyssal Scourge → Leng blades |

**Fresh-account default for leveling:** prefer **2H / halberd-type** when stacking mobs (Abyss, Shattered Worlds, Vyres). Switch to **DW** for bosses and single-target when you can afford Drygores or better.

**Key replacement ability:** [Adaptive Strike](https://runescape.wiki/w/Adaptive_Strike) (7 Attack) replaced **Cleave** and **Decimate**. On 2H it hits a **cone** (main target + up to 8); on DW it is a double hit. Put it on every training bar.

### Settings checklist

1. Action bar cog → **Auto-setup action bar** for a starter Melee bar, then customise.
2. Enable **Target Information** so you can see hit chance.
3. Prefer monsters **weak to melee** (and your weapon type when listed).
4. Chat: `/wiki <item>` for live Wiki pages.

## Leveling route (1–120)

Fresh-account path. Optional upgrades are marked **Upgrade when affordable**.

### Levels 1–30 — Quests, then easy melee

**Best start:** complete early melee XP quests before grinding.

| Quest | Why |
|-------|-----|
| [Waterfall Quest](https://runescape.wiki/w/Waterfall_Quest) | Huge early Attack + Strength XP (commonly jumps you toward the high 20s / 30) |
| [Tree Gnome Village](https://runescape.wiki/w/Tree_Gnome_Village), [Fight Arena](https://runescape.wiki/w/Fight_Arena) | More Attack XP with low requirements |
| [Scorpion Catcher](https://runescape.wiki/w/Scorpion_Catcher), [Haunted Mine](https://runescape.wiki/w/Haunted_Mine) | Strength XP |
| [Witch's House](https://runescape.wiki/w/Witch%27s_House) | Early Constitution |

**Grind fillers if needed:**

| Levels | Target | Location | Notes |
|--------|--------|----------|-------|
| 1–20 | Chickens / Troll Cave | Lumbridge / Burthorpe | AFK; buy bronze→iron gear in shops |
| 20–30 | Rock Crabs / Hill Giants | Fremennik north / Edgeville Dungeon | Upgrade to **mithril** weapons |

**Gear (fresh):** bronze → iron → steel → mithril armour; **halberd** of the same metal if you can afford it (better AoE later).  
**DW option:** scimitar / longsword + off-hand of the same tier.

**Key unlocks:** **Assault** at **3 Attack**; **Adaptive Strike** at **7 Attack**. Keep both on the bar after Auto-setup.

| Method | Notes |
|--------|-------|

**Gear targets:**

- **Armour:** Masterwork → Trimmed Masterwork; **Vestments of Havoc** is the expensive power endgame set

### Levels 99–120 — Cap push

Combat Modernisation uses a **stronger mid-level damage curve** (logarithmic scaling) — you do not need 120 to start bosses. 120 unlocks mastery cape perks:

| Skill | 120 mastery perk (Wiki) |
|-------|-------------------------|
| **Attack** | **+2% hit chance** on Melee attacks |
| **Strength** | **Dismember / Slaughter / Massacre** heal an **additional 3%** of damage dealt |

**Training:** keep using Vyres, high Slayer, Abyss, or bossing (GWD2, Arch-Glacor, ED trash) — XP from damage still scales with your kill speed. Use **Inspire Awe** (Archaeology) and other XP boosts when available.

## Ability bars (Revolution vs Manual)

Right-click the action bar cog → **Auto-setup** for a legal starter bar, then tune.

### Mental model

1. Basics (**Adaptive Strike**, **Rend**, etc.) build adrenaline **and** Bloodlust  
2. At **4 Bloodlust**, fire **Assault** (single-target) or **Hurricane** / **Flurry** (AoE / DW)  
3. **Berserk** (76 Attack) for burst windows — you take more damage; bring sustain  
4. **Overpower** (15 Attack, **60%** adrenaline baseline) as your main dump; **Igneous Kal-Ket** makes it a **double hit** (280–340% ×2)  
5. Bleeds: **Dismember** (50) → **Slaughter** (60) → **Massacre** (75) — multi-cast line with built-in healing  
6. **Meteor Strike** (90) — AoE ultimate that also buffs Melee adrenaline generation for 30s  

### Suggested 2H AoE training bar (Revolution basics + manual spenders)

Prioritise: **Adaptive Strike**, **Hurricane**, **Assault**, **Berserk**, **Meteor Strike**, **Overpower**, bleeds.  
Do **not** look for **Cleave**, **Quake**, or **Smash** — they are gone.

### Suggested DW single-target bar

Prioritise: **Flurry / Greater Flurry**, **Assault**, **Rend**, **Adaptive Strike**, **Berserk**, **Overpower**, bleeds.  
Use DW for bosses; swap to 2H when stacking many targets.

> [!TIP]
> **Basics-only Revolution** (automate basics; press Assault / Hurricane / Flurry / Berserk / Overpower / Meteor Strike yourself) is the best learning path after Combat Modernisation.

### Ek-ZekKil — current kit (do not use 2024–early-2025 bleed guides)

As of the **May/June 2025 rework** and **Combat Modernisation** cleanup ([Ek-ZekKil](https://runescape.wiki/w/Ek-ZekKil)):

- Special is **Igneous Showdown** (marks **Flamebound Rival**; big multi-hit when re-cast on the same rival) — **not** the old Igneous Cleave bleed
- Passive **Ashen Vow** is rival damage taken/dealt — it no longer buffs removed abilities (**Quake / Smash**)
- Requires **95 Strength** (not Attack) to wield
- Can be upgraded toward T100 with a **Shard of Genesis Essence**
- Old “store EZK in EoF + camp Masterwork Spear to extend Cleave bleed” guides are **outdated**

## Endgame DPS notes (Berserk-centric)

> Optional deep dive for players with T90+ gear. Fresh accounts should finish the leveling route first.  
> Practical framing cross-checked against community endgame DPS guides (e.g. post–Combat Modernisation Melee DPS videos) and Wiki pages linked below. Numbers follow the Wiki.

### Big picture

Build the fight around **[Berserk](https://runescape.wiki/w/Berserk)** (76 Attack):

1. Prep adrenaline (**[Meteor Strike](https://runescape.wiki/w/Meteor_Strike)** helps — 4.5% adren every tick for 30s while Melee is equipped)
2. **Berserk** → dump **Bloodlust** spenders + **Overpower**
3. Filler until Berserk is ready again (often a **Zamorak godsword** special / EoF — ~25% damage in a planted area for ~20s)
4. Repeat

With **[Vestments of Havoc](https://runescape.wiki/w/Vestments_of_havoc_armour)** (3+ pieces), Berserk lasts **~26.4s** instead of **~20.4s**. **[Greater Flurry](https://runescape.wiki/w/Greater_Flurry)** extends active Berserk by **1 tick per hit** (channelled), so two Flurries inside Berserk add a large chunk of uptime — still not a full minute, so you need a filler plan.

### High-value basics (beyond Adaptive Strike / Rend)

| Ability | Level / unlock | Why it matters |
|---------|----------------|----------------|
| **[Punish](https://runescape.wiki/w/Punish)** | 60 Attack | **2.5×** damage when the target is below **50%** HP — keep it on the bar for executes |
| **[Greater Fury](https://runescape.wiki/w/Greater_Fury)** / Fury | 21+ Attack (Greater via codex) | Guarantees a **critical** on your next ability — set up Overpower / Hurricane / big specs |
| **[Greater Barge](https://runescape.wiki/w/Greater_Barge)** | 65 Attack (codex) | After waiting off-target (~4.8s+), your next **Assault / Flurry / Greater Flurry** becomes a **DoT** so you can weave other abilities while it ticks |
| **[Chaos Roar](https://runescape.wiki/w/Chaos_Roar)** | 92 Attack (Zamorak codex) | Next Melee ability within **7.2s** deals **1.75×** base damage — pair with **Mercy dagger** EoF or **EZK** Showdown, **not** Overpower (hitcap waste) |
| **Backhand** | 31 Attack (2 charges) | Stun utility; strong with **Flanking** perk when you can stand behind the target |
| **Bladed Dive** | DW only | Niche movement / AoE; most players keybind non-combat **Dive** instead |

**[Gloves of Passage](https://runescape.wiki/w/Gloves_of_passage)** (Rend trigger after modernisation): next Melee hit within 6s deals **+10%** damage (**+16%** enhanced + Enchantment of agony); target takes **+20%** bleed damage for 10s (**+25%** enchanted). Use Rend before a big hit or bleed dump.

### Enhanced spenders (quick rules)

| Ability | Use when |
|---------|----------|
| **Assault** | Default Bloodlust dump; 6s CD; channelled — avoid if you need a free GCD for a defensive |
| **Hurricane** (2H) | AoE, or single-target when you need **instant** damage + free GCD (mechanics / defensives); CD shrinks **3s per enemy hit** |
| **Flurry / Greater Flurry** (DW) | Berserk extend + Bloodlust execute below ~**61%** HP (see [Bloodlust](https://runescape.wiki/w/Bloodlust) comparison) |
| **Dismember line** | Spear on → mash until Massacre is spent → swap back; MW Spear = **+50%** bleed duration |

### Ultimates

| Ultimate | Role |
|----------|------|
| **Overpower** | Main Berserk dump; **60%** adren; Igneous cape → **two hits**; Berserk shortens CD to **9s**. Do **not** Chaos Roar this under hitcap |
| **[Pulverise](https://runescape.wiki/w/Pulverise)** (2H, 71 Attack) | Single big hit; applies **Pulverised** so the target **deals 25% less damage** for 30s; **+50% adren on killing blow** — strong for trash / phase kills |
| **Meteor Strike** | Adren engine + AoE; open Berserk windows with it when you can |
| **Berserk** | Defines Melee — plan the whole kill around its uptime |

### Specials / EoFs (shortlist)

| Tool | Role |
|------|------|
| **Leng** (Shard + Sliver) | Build **Primordial Ice** stacks on hits; special consumes stacks for Hurricane-like damage and can become very cheap at ~3+ stacks |
| **Ek-ZekKil** | First Showdown **marks** Flamebound Rival; second Showdown on the mark = multi-hit nuke (often Chaos Roar’d). Camp the physical sword for Ashen Vow while marked |
| **Mercy dagger** (EoF) | Popular Chaos Roar target — three hits in one GCD with ramping crit |
| **Zamorak godsword** (wield or EoF) | Planted **+25%** Melee damage zone (~20s) between Berserks |
| **Annihilation / Gravitate** (EoF) | Situational +% damage stacks; cancels if you swap weapons mid-buff — niche prebuilds |

Skip equilibrium-specific EoF swaps unless you already run that setup.

### Example Berserk window (pattern, not a boss script)

1. Stall / bank adren → **Meteor Strike** → **Anticipate** (optional) → **Berserk**
2. **Greater Barge** (charged) → **Assault** or **Greater Flurry** (DoT weave) → **Overpower**
3. **Rend** (Gloves of Passage) → **Overpower** / **Assault** while Bloodlust is up
4. **Chaos Roar** → **Mercy dagger** *or* marked **EZK Showdown** (pick one big nuke)
5. More **Overpower** + **Flurry** (Berserk extend) → **Hurricane** if you need a free GCD
6. Berserk ends → **ZGS** filler + basics / Assault → rebuild → next Berserk

Bosses with immune windows: prefer **Hurricane** over **Assault** so you can still spend Bloodlust without locking into a long channel.

## Short boss starter

You do **not** need 120 to begin. Aim for ~80+ Melee stats, Soul Split, and T80–T90 weapons before serious GWD2.

### Heart of Gielinor (GWD2)

Entry is boostable around **80** in the relevant style.

> [!NOTE]
> As of **9 July 2026**, Jagex has **previewed** a Mid-Game Rebalance that would remove gold instance costs for most bosses (with keystone exceptions such as Ascensions). Treat that as **upcoming**, not guaranteed live — check the Wiki / patch notes the day you camp.

| Boss | Why Melee works | Starter tip |
|------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Twin Furies** | Shared HP pool — **AoE doubles up**; Wiki recommends Melee | Bring 2H AoE (**Adaptive Strike** + Hurricane); Barge to chase ceiling collapse; save big spenders for bomb windows |
| **Vindicta & Gorvek** | Straightforward melee autos; learn two phases | Halberd range helps positioning; respect phase-2 fire walls |
| **Gregorovic** | Melee can stay outside his melee range with **halberd-type** weapons | Turn **run off** if you keep walking into melee distance; pray correctly vs melee/ranged swaps |
| **Helwyr** | Common Melee learning boss | Learn mushroom / cleave patterns; don’t panic-eat every hit |

**Fresh-account GWD2 kit:** Bandos or better, Laniakea’s / Nox scythe or Drygores, Overloads or extremes, Soul Split, food + emergency thralls/familiar, **Vampyrism Aspect** (or Penance Aspect if prayer is the bottleneck).

## What “proficient Melee” looks like

**Competent mid-game**

- 90+ Attack/Strength, 80+ Defence, 92+ Prayer, Overloads, Invention started  
- Nox scythe **or** Drygores, Bandos/Torva/Masterwork  
- Comfortable Bloodlust loop: Adaptive Strike/Rend → Assault/Hurricane/Flurry  
- GWD2 kills without panic  

**Strong endgame**

- 120 Attack/Strength, 95+ Prayer, 96+ Herblore, BiS-leaning perks  
- MW2H / Tumeken's Light / EZK / Leng + Vestments or Trimmed Masterwork  
- Igneous Kal-Ket, Archaeology relics, Chaos Roar + Greater Barge when affordable  
- Manual Berserk windows (Meteor → Berserk → Overpower / Flurry / Chaos Roar nukes → ZGS filler); EZK via **Igneous Showdown**
